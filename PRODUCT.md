# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

static HTML5, CSS3, and native JavaScript ES6+. No framework, backend, database, CMS, login, or large animation library.

## Users

Primary visitors are people exploring Chinese traditional color through culture, artifacts, seasonal rhythm, ritual order, and aesthetic context. They arrive in a browser and should understand the museum's premise through a short, immersive scroll.

## Product Purpose

The product is a static digital museum named "中国色彩数字博物馆". It uses color as the interface language and narrative structure so visitors can move from sensing color, to understanding the five-color system, to entering themed exhibition halls.

## Positioning

This is not a general traditional-culture website or a simple color-card collection. Its distinct mechanism is using Chinese traditional color as spatial language, interaction language, and cultural information architecture.

## Operating Context

The site opens directly from local files or a simple static server. The home prologue introduces the world through the sequence "墨起 -> 五色初醒 -> 五色认知 -> 循色入境 -> 今日国色 -> 入馆".

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed scope for the current build is only the home prologue page, `index.html`. Exhibition pages are link targets but are not developed until the user supplies their prompts. Required interactions include restrained custom cursor on desktop, scroll reveal, page color-curtain transition, five-color exploration, exhibition entrances, deterministic color of the day, random color switching, and HEX/RGB copy feedback without alerts.

## Brand Commitments

Name: 中国色彩数字博物馆. English support label: CHINESE COLOR DIGITAL MUSEUM. Core line: 色载华夏 · 数藏千年. The voice is restrained, accurate, literary, and museum-like. The visual world must be Eastern minimal, poetic, spacious, contemporary, and grounded in mineral-like traditional colors.

## Product Principles

Color is the museum's structure, not decoration.

Traditional culture should guide layout, rhythm, and interaction rather than appear as pasted motifs.

The interface must feel like a modern digital museum: spacious, calm, precise, and usable.

Every interaction should be legible without becoming theatrical.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic headings, real links and buttons, visible focus states, accessible color contrast, mobile touch targets of at least 44px, and `prefers-reduced-motion` support.
